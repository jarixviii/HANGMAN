Lamplight Hangman

Guess the hidden word before the lamp burns out.

A word is chosen at random and shown as blank tiles. Pick letters one at a time. A right letter appears everywhere it occurs. A wrong letter adds a piece to the gallows. Reveal the word before the drawing is finished and you score points.
